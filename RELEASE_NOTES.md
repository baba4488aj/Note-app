# Lumenwild 0.10.0

- Sort your collection by element, role, name, or collection order. Combine element and role filters; your choices are saved.
- One account level now controls every pet’s stats, skill unlocks, and growth stage. New recruits and custom pets immediately join at that level.
- Existing saves keep the highest pet level you reached and preserve progress toward the next level. Your collection, skill builds, equipment, and tower progress carry over.
- Higher-level enemies give more XP. Each kill awards account XP once, including reaction, burn, counter, and passive kills. The account level cap remains 100.
- Combine elemental attacks in either order: Steam burst, Wildfire, Overload, Bloom, Conduction, and Static bind. Watch enemy auras and reaction previews to plan your next move.
- Reactions have dedicated visual effects, and auto-training considers them when choosing skills.

Installed Windows copies download this update automatically. Open **Saves → Game updates → Check for updates**, then choose **Restart & update** when ready, or close the game to install after saving.

The Windows installer is unsigned. Existing desktop saves and pre-update backups remain in your Windows profile.
